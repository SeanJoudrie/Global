Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

Globalio (“we”, “the app”) is a free flags & geography game. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

  • Game progress, stored on your device. Your streaks, scores, learned flags, settings and similar progress are saved locally in your browser (using localStorage). This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • No accounts. Globalio has no sign-up or login, and we do not ask for your name, email, or other personal details.
  • Basic technical data. Like any website, our host (Netlify) and third parties may process standard request information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site.

Advertising

Globalio may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • For more on third-party cookies and opting out, see aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.

Images and third-party content

Flag and emblem images are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their respective licenses. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Globalio is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Your choices

You can clear all locally-stored game data at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for Globalio. You can manage or block cookies through your browser settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above.
